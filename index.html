Story

Rook Vale slips through a secret portal into Moo-topia, a floating night jungle run by the rudest party cows alive.

How to Play

  • Phone: drag the left side to move, drag the right side to look. Tap Hit, Jump (tap again in the air to double jump) and Dash (you can't be hit mid-dash).
  • Keyboard: WASD or arrows to move, Space to jump, J or click to attack, K or Shift to dash, Q and E to turn the camera, 1, 2 and 3 to switch weapons, 4, 5, 6 and 7 to cast spells, Esc to pause, R to scan.
  • Weapons: Energy Blade (fast 3-hit chain), Glow Spear (long reach, heavy thrust), Moonstaff (wide sweeps and a spinning finisher that sends cows flying).
  • Cows fight back. When their spots glow orange and they paw the ground, a charge is coming. Dash sideways, then punish them while they're dizzy.
  • Knock cows into the glowing corral to impound them, or off a cliff for a Yeet bonus.
  • Pink clouds bounce you high. Rainbow bridges connect the islands.
  • Collect Glow Milk to heal, stars for points, and Glitter from cows, combos, bosses and hidden sparkle caches. Spend Glitter in the shop on looks. Shop items never affect stats.
  • Loot: cows drop gear in five rarities, shown by beam color: Common (white), Uncommon (green), Rare (blue), Epic (purple) and Legendary (gold). Walk up and tap Pick up (or press F). Bosses always drop Epic or better. Open Bag (or press I) to compare and equip gear, sell junk for Glitter, and brew potions from crafting bits. Drink a potion with the potion button or H.
  • Ultra Rare: a sixth tier above Legendary with a rainbow beam and its own sound. Any cow has a tiny chance to drop one. King Cudsworth has a much better chance. Glitter find gear raises the odds.
  • Levels: every cow you bonk gives XP. Level up for more health, damage and mana, plus a talent point to spend in the Bag. Better gear needs a higher level.
  • Spells: tap the spell buttons (or press 4 to 7). Glow Milk Mend heals, Moonbeam Stampede blasts everything nearby, Cowbell Courage boosts damage and armor, Udder Thunder chains lightning. Spells use mana, which refills over time, and unlock as you level up.
  • Buffs: tonics, glowing shrines and spells give timed buffs. Their icons and timers sit under your health bar.
  • The King: beat the Baroness to reach the Udder Throne, where King Cudsworth fights in three phases. Watch for his royal decree: three shockwaves in a row.
